# Capital Current

A research terminal for seeing where venture funding is being announced. Its main action is moving from industry totals to the rounds and original sources behind them.

Color: ink #17243d, canvas #f5f7fb, paper #ffffff, iris #6554c0, teal #267765, amber #8d611d. A quiet navy navigation rail grounds a bright, data-dense workspace. Violet identifies capital, teal identifies a positive sample change; neither implies returns.

Type: Avenir Next for headings, the system sans-serif for reading, SF Mono for dates and tabular utility figures. Display type is restrained; tables and charts have precedence over promotional copy.

Layout: fixed left navigation and compact top bar; title and explicit coverage context; period controls; five plain metric columns; a wide daily activity chart next to an industry allocation chart; an industry ranking table; an accessible slide-over for source-level inspection. On narrow screens navigation becomes a horizontal band and charts stack. A compact sector allocation ribbon is the signature: click a segment to filter to its industry.

Rejected direction: an imitation trading screen with glowing fake tickers and fictional price movement. This product measures announcements, not prices or returns. No decorative numbering, thin left rules framing text, stock photography, cropped assets, decorative sparkline data, or licensed external component collections. Use lettermarks for entity identification without claiming official logos.

Use real data only. Keep source status, date, sampled coverage, missing values, non-USD treatment, deduplication, and excluded financings inspectable. All filtering, tables, charts, export and drilldowns must use the same normalized dataset. Unknown is never zero.

Verification: rendered views at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320px; focus, keyboard/Escape, mobile navigation, filters, date boundaries, empty states, reduced motion, duplicate merging and incomplete amount denominators. No deployment is implied by a local preview.
